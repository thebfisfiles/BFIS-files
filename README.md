## 4. Signs of concealment, corruption risk, and defective case handling

The facts below establish recordkeeping and accountability anomalies. 

### 4.1 Every complaint is closed; parent-feedback status is frequently entered almost immediately

Counts:

| Metric | Count |
|---|---:|
| Total complaints | 505 |
| Complaints with comp_status=5 (closed) | 505 |
| Feedback “Not Responded” | 457 |
| Feedback “Satisfied” | 40 |
| Feedback “Not Satisfied” | 8 |
| “Not Responded” entered within 60 seconds of the last status-3 response | 181 |
| “Not Responded” entered within 24 hours of the last status-3 response | 270 |

The exact 60-second calculation uses the last recorded status-3 response and first “Not Responded” status-4 timestamp for each complaint. A meaningful parent response opportunity is difficult to reconcile with 181 “Not Responded” entries appearing within 60 seconds unless the workflow was automated or recorded retrospectively.

The pattern appears in safeguarding cases. Complaints 104 and 105, both school-marked valid against Areej Minhas, were marked “Not Responded” and then closed. Complaints 1540 and 1548 were also marked “Not Responded” despite meetings and substantive actions. This makes the feedback field unreliable as proof that parents declined to respond.

### 4.2 Closed tickets ending at an interim response

Support-ticket counts:

- 8,227 total support tickets.
- 7,313 closed tickets (st_open=0).
- 44 closed tickets have no status entry.
- 4,811 closed tickets have only one status entry.
- 113 closed tickets end with interim language promising investigation, findings, an update, or later contact, with no later status.
- A focused safeguarding-term screen found 40 closed high-risk tickets; 13 end at an interim response.

Case-specific examples in this packet:

| Ticket | Final response | State |

Closure does not prove no offline work occurred. It does establish that the parent-facing audit trail is incomplete. In each of the listed safeguarding tickets the school closed the matter at the interim stage without recording a final finding or action in the ticket history.

### 4.3 Internal findings omitted from parent-facing records

- Ticket 17008 ends with an interim promise. Two days later, violation 18804 records CCTV and staff corroboration, private-area touching, distress/hurt, suspension, an undertaking, and a related file. None of that finding appears in the ticket history.
- Ticket 16645 tells the parent only that action was taken. Violation 18660 separately records CCTV-related detail, suspension, an undertaking, and a related file.

- Areej Minhas’s parent-facing complaint responses repeatedly use generic language such as “proper action has been taken”; the more specific warning, deduction, and final-warning descriptions appear only in HR-file metadata.

This separation may result from fragmented application design, but it has the practical effect of concealing the specific finding or sanction from a reviewer who sees only the parent-facing case. The school repeatedly failed to place the concrete disciplinary outcome into the parent-facing record.
